01 — The Workshop

From foundations

A 10-day capacity-building programme bringing together ecology, evolution, genomics and conservation education.

The Faculty Training Workshop on Inculcating Conservation Thinking through Ecology and Evolution Education in India is a 10-day capacity-building programme organized at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, from 7th–17th December 2026.
